# GeoIP attribution

Russian country ranges in `geoip/ru/ipv4.txt` and `ipv6.txt` are derived from
[DB-IP IP to Country Lite](https://db-ip.com/db/lite.php), November 2026.
The derived RU portion of `geoip.dat` is provided under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Changes: selected country RU, converted inclusive address ranges into CIDR prefixes.
The DB-IP data is not covered by the repository's MIT license.
IP geolocation can be inaccurate; manual additions are stored separately.
